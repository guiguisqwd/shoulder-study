Check yourself before moving on 先自测，再往下读

Supraspinatus inserts onto which facet of the greater tubercle?冈上肌止于大结节的哪一个小面？Show answer · 展开答案

The superior facet.

上小面。

Infraspinatus and teres minor both externally rotate the humerus. Which nerve supplies each?冈下肌和小圆肌都外旋肱骨，各由什么神经支配？Show answer · 展开答案

Infraspinatus: suprascapular nerve (usually C5–C6). Teres minor: axillary nerve (mainly C5–C6).

冈下肌：肩胛上神经（通常 C5–C6）；小圆肌：腋神经（主要 C5–C6）。

Which cuff muscle inserts on the lesser tubercle, and what is its main action?哪块肩袖肌止于小结节？主要动作是什么？Show answer · 展开答案

Subscapularis; internal rotation of the humerus.

肩胛下肌；使肱骨内旋。

Is teres major part of the rotator cuff?大圆肌属于肩袖吗？Show answer · 展开答案

No. Teres major is a separate muscle.

不属于，大圆肌是另一块肌肉。

Take-home · 本章要点
Three on the greater tubercle (S, I, T: top to bottom), one on the lesser (subscapularis); all C5–C6.三块止于大结节（冈上、冈下、小圆，从上到下），一块止于小结节（肩胛下肌）；都是 C5–C6。
