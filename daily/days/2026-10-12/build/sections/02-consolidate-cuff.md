### Self-test｜自测

#### Check yourself｜先自测

**Q.** Supraspinatus inserts onto which facet of the greater tubercle?<br>冈上肌止于大结节的哪一个小面？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

The superior facet.<br>上小面。

</details>

**Q.** Infraspinatus and teres minor both externally rotate the humerus. Which nerve supplies each?<br>冈下肌和小圆肌都外旋肱骨，各由什么神经支配？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Infraspinatus: suprascapular nerve (usually C5–C6). Teres minor: axillary nerve (mainly C5–C6).<br>冈下肌：肩胛上神经（通常 C5–C6）；小圆肌：腋神经（主要 C5–C6）。

</details>

**Q.** Which cuff muscle inserts on the lesser tubercle, and what is its main action?<br>哪块肩袖肌止于小结节？主要动作是什么？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Subscapularis; internal rotation of the humerus.<br>肩胛下肌；使肱骨内旋。

</details>

**Q.** Is teres major part of the rotator cuff?<br>大圆肌属于肩袖吗？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

No. Teres major is a separate muscle.<br>不属于，大圆肌是另一块肌肉。

</details>

**Q.** Fill in: The infraspinatus originates from the ____ fossa, passes behind the glenohumeral joint, and inserts onto the ____ facet of the greater tubercle.<br>填空：冈下肌起自____窝，经盂肱关节后方，止于大结节____小面。

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Infraspinous; middle.<br>冈下窝；中。

</details>

**Q.** Look at figure 04: name the three muscles on the posterior view from top to bottom.<br>看图 04：后面观从上到下是哪三块肌肉？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

Supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor.<br>冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌。

</details>

**Q.** Which of today's new acupoints lies over a rotator cuff muscle, and which muscle?<br>今天的新穴位里，哪一个的深面是肩袖肌？是哪块？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

TE15 Tianliao; supraspinatus lies deep to trapezius there.<br>天髎；斜方肌深面是冈上肌。

</details>

**Q.** Which spinal segments do all four cuff muscles share?<br>四块肩袖肌共同的脊髓节段是什么？

<details><summary>Answer 答案</summary>

C5–C6 (subscapularis: some sources include C7).<br>C5–C6（肩胛下肌部分资料还包括 C7）。

</details>


### Flashcards｜记忆卡

- **肩外俞 Jiānwàishū**（定位？层次？）→ Below T1 spinous process, 3 cun lateral to the posterior midline<br>在背部，第1胸椎棘突下，后正中线旁开3寸（GB/T 12346-2021）。层次：皮肤 → 皮下组织（第1胸神经后支内侧皮支）→ 斜方肌（副神经）→ 肩胛提肌、小菱形肌（肩胛背神经；颈横动、静脉）→ 深面胸壁、胸膜与肺
- **肩中俞 Jiānzhōngshū**（定位？层次？）→ Below C7 spinous process, 2 cun lateral to the posterior midline<br>在背部，第7颈椎棘突下，后正中线旁开2寸（GB/T 12346-2021）。层次：皮肤 → 皮下组织（第1胸神经后支内侧皮支）→ 斜方肌（副神经）→ 肩胛提肌（肩胛背神经；颈横动、静脉）→ 深面胸壁上口、胸膜顶
- **天髎 Tiānliáo**（定位？层次？）→ In the depression at the superior angle of the scapula<br>在肩带部，肩胛骨上角骨际凹陷中（GB/T 12346-2021）。层次：皮肤 → 皮下组织（第1胸神经后支外侧皮支）→ 斜方肌（副神经；颈横动脉降支）→ 冈上肌（肩胛上动脉、肩胛上神经肌支）→ 肩胛骨上角附近骨面
- **臂臑 Bìnào**（定位？层次？）→ Lateral arm on the LI11–LI15 line, 7 cun above LI11, just anterior to the deltoid border<br>在臂外侧，在曲池（LI11）与肩髃（LI15）连线上，三角肌前缘处（GB/T 12346-2021）。层次：皮肤 → 皮下组织（前臂后皮神经等，教材）→ 三角肌下端前缘（腋神经）→ 深层有桡神经本干（教材）
- **Supraspinatus**（冈上肌）→ The supraspinatus originates from the supraspinous fossa of the scapula, passes laterally beneath the acromion as a tendon, and inserts onto the superior facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus.<br>神经：肩胛上神经（通常为 C5–C6）；动作：它参与肩关节外展，并帮助将肱骨头稳定在关节盂内。
- **Infraspinatus**（冈下肌）→ The infraspinatus originates from the infraspinous fossa of the scapula, passes laterally behind the glenohumeral joint as its fibers converge into a tendon, and inserts onto the middle facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus.<br>神经：肩胛上神经（通常为 C5–C6）；动作：它使肱骨外旋，并参与盂肱关节的动态稳定。
- **Teres minor**（小圆肌）→ The teres minor originates from the upper part of the lateral border of the scapula, passes superolaterally along the inferior border of the infraspinatus, and inserts onto the inferior facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus.<br>神经：腋神经（主要为 C5–C6）；动作：它协助肱骨外旋并帮助稳定肱骨头。大圆肌是另一块肌肉，不属于肩袖。
- **Subscapularis**（肩胛下肌）→ The subscapularis originates from the subscapular fossa on the anterior surface of the scapula, passes laterally in front of the glenohumeral joint, and inserts onto the lesser tubercle of the humerus.<br>神经：上、下肩胛下神经（C5–C6；部分资料还包括 C7）；动作：它使肱骨内旋，并参与盂肱关节前方的稳定。
- **The rotator cuff: S I T S**（肩袖四肌：S I T S）→ Supraspinatus, Infraspinatus, Teres minor, Subscapularis<br>冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌、肩胛下肌
- **Greater tubercle: superior, middle, inferior facets**（大结节的上、中、下三个小面）→ Supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor (top to bottom)<br>从上到下：冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌
